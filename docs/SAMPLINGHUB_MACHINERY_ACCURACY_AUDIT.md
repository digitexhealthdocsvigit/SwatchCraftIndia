## 1. Business Operating Model & Compliance Foundation

SamplingHub (Digitex Studio) is an **India-based managed OEM fabric sampling solutions provider established in 2011**, with centralized operations and coordination based in Thane-Bhiwandi, PIN: 421302, Maharashtra, India.

### Mandatory Compliance Principles:
1. **No Claims of Owned Factories:** SamplingHub does **not** own or operate a factory building in Bhiwandi.
2. **No Claims of Owned Machinery:** SamplingHub does **not** own Polytex automated machines, computerized cutting gantries, automated serging systems, or high-speed automated pinking lines.
3. **Managed OEM Partner Network:** Production is executed through a vetted network of specialized partner manufacturing units in Maharashtra. SamplingHub manages:
   - Technical product development, CAD layouts, and structural engineering
   - Fabric roll receipt, moisture verification, and material staging
   - Precision partner workflow allocation and production supervision
   - Centralized multi-stage quality control inspection (100% manual piece-by-piece inspection)
   - Export-grade moisture-barrier packaging and palletization
   - International air and sea freight logistics coordination (JNPT / BOM to global ports)
4. **Authentic Capability Descriptors:** All production processes are represented accurately as:
   - *Manual fabric sample preparation*
   - *Manual cutting and sample handling*
   - *Sample book assembly and rigid board casing*
   - *Fabric swatch mounting and presentation*
   - *Custom sample card preparation and die-cutting*
   - *Quality inspection (dimensional tolerances, alignment, label serialization)*
   - *Finished-product packing and dispatch coordination*
